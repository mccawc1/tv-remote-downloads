# TV Remote downloads

Your phone or PC becomes the remote for your smart TV. Works with LG (webOS), Roku and Samsung (Tizen) TVs over your home Wi-Fi.

Get the newest files on the [Releases page](https://github.com/mccawc1/tv-remote-downloads/releases/latest):

- **TVRemote-android.apk**: Android 8.0 and newer.
- **TVRemote.exe**: Windows 10 and 11. One file, nothing to install.

## Free and Couch Potato

The app is free to use. The free version shows three TV app buttons (YouTube, Netflix, Hulu), and the Android app shows a small ad banner.

**Couch Potato** is the paid version: add, remove and reorder the TV app buttons, and no ads. It is unlocked by a key you type in Settings, on the Buttons tab. The same key works on your phone and your PC.

## Install

**Android:** download the APK, open it, tap Install. If the phone asks, allow installs from your browser. If Play Protect warns you, tap More details, then Install anyway.

**Windows:** download TVRemote.exe and double-click it. If a blue box says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.

Then make sure the phone or PC is on the same Wi-Fi as the TV, pick your TV, and press Accept on the TV the first time it asks.

This repository holds built files only.
